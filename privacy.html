Privacy Policy: Planet Mars Stock Exchange (PMSE)

Effective: November 23, 2026
Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Calvin Job Driggers ("we", "us") handles information in the Planet Mars Stock Exchange game ("PMSE", "the app"), including the iOS app and the web version.

Summary

  • Your game is saved on your device. In the current version we do not run servers that receive your game data.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your card number or payment details.
  • We use no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal information.
  • PMSE is for players 20 and older (rated 18+ on the App Store).
  • You can delete your data at any time with Account Reset in the app, or by deleting the app.

Information the app handles

When you play, the app creates and stores the following on your device so the game works:

  • Age and privacy confirmation: that you confirmed you are 20 or older and read this policy (a yes/no check, not your birthdate).
  • Player profile: your player name (for example "Player 1") and avatar choice.
  • Names you type: first names and cities you give your in-game friends, and the company names you create. Please don't enter real people's full names or real company names.
  • Game progress: day, in-game cash and holdings, companies listed, IPOs, hotel and weekend progress, so you can resume where you left off.
  • Session times: when a session started, how long you have played, when you were logged out, and when the 10-hour wait ends. These enforce the 2-hour play limit, the 5-minute rest warning and the 10-hour wait, and are kept separately for each player profile on the device.
  • Purchase records: which Earth calls were completed, so each purchase adds exactly one company.

Under Apple's definitions, information processed only on your device and never sent to us is not "collected" by us.

If we add online accounts or cloud saves

We may later add an optional online account or cloud save, for example so purchased companies survive a new phone. If we do:

  • The items above (player name, a user ID, game progress, session times, purchase records, and an email address if login uses one) would be stored on our servers or with a hosting provider acting for us, only to run the game.
  • We would update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that feature ships, and tell you in the app.
  • We would still not sell your information, track you across other apps or websites, or use it for advertising.

Purchases

The Earth call is an optional in-app purchase ($9.99 in the U.S.; local prices elsewhere) of a virtual item that adds one company to your exchange. Payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card number, billing address, or Apple ID password. Apple may give the app basic transaction information (such as a transaction ID, the product and the date) so we can deliver the item. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • PMSE may show in-game ad placements (for example a poster in the Mars Hotel lobby). These are images built into the game. The current sample brand, "Phobos Patty Co.", is fictional. Showing a placement sends no information about you to any brand. If this ever changes, we will update this policy first.

Sale and sharing of information

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not disclose it to third parties except (a) Apple, as needed to process purchases, (b) service providers acting for us if online features are added, or (c) when required by law.

Age requirement and children's privacy

PMSE is intended for adults. The game asks you to confirm you are 20 or older before you can play, and the App Store listing is rated 18+. PMSE is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 (or under 13 as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, or under 16 in the EU/UK). If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: use Account Reset / New Game in the app to erase your profile, progress, session times and purchase records, or delete the app. Note that this also removes companies you bought. Apple does not restore consumable purchases.
  • If online features are added: you can delete your account in the app or by emailing us. We will delete your server data within 30 days unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information:

  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither). We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.
  • EU / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): the rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Where we process data, we rely on performance of our contract with you (running the game) and our legitimate interest in keeping it working and fair (for example the session limits).
  • Other regions: similar rights may apply under local law.

Because game data stays on your device today, most requests can be completed yourself with Account Reset. For anything else, email us. We may need to verify the request.

Security

On-device data is protected by your device's security. If online features are added, we will use reasonable safeguards such as encryption in transit. No method is 100% secure.

International users

If online features are added, data may be processed in the United States or other countries where our providers operate, with appropriate safeguards where required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
