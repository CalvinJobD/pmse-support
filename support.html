Common questions

  • Why was I logged out? Each session is up to 2 hours of play, with a 5-minute rest warning. After that the desk closes for 10 hours. Your progress is saved, and you pick up right where you left off.
  • What does an Earth call do? It's an optional in-app purchase ($9.99 in the U.S.) that adds exactly one company to your exchange. The exchange holds up to 2,500 companies, and reaching 2,500 ends the game with a Player 1 win.
  • How do I get a refund? App Store purchases are refunded by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • I got a new phone or reinstalled. Where are my companies? Your game is saved on your device. Apple does not restore consumable purchases, so deleting the app removes saved progress.
  • How do I delete my data? Use Account Reset / New Game in the app, or delete the app. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Is any real money or stock involved? No real securities are traded. In-game shares, IPOs and currency have no real-world value. Earth calls are real purchases of virtual in-game items only.
